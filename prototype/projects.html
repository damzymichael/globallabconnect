This initiative focused on institutionalizing standard operational rigor and preventive maintenance protocols for high-throughput diagnostic equipment. The program addressed recurring analyzer downtime, uncalibrated assay variability, and gaps in documentation required for ISO 15189 compliance. Through structured equipment technical transfers, staff re-skilling, and routine internal audit simulations, the project transformed regional diagnostic hubs into internationally credible testing reference points.

build_circle Key Interventions & Scope of Work

  • check Diagnostic Tech Transfer: Procurement facilitation, vendor qualification, and commissioning of high-accuracy automated analyzers.
  • check Preventive Maintenance Systems: Calibration schedule deployment, cold-chain monitoring integration, and spare part pipeline management.
  • check ISO 15189 Quality Roadmap: Drafting standardized quality manuals, non-conformance management processes, and pre-audit readiness trials.
  • check Performance Verification: Establishing strict analytical sensitivity and specificity benchmarks across all active testing menus.

calendar_today Timeline: [Date / Timeline]

In response to recurrent biological threats and accelerating antimicrobial resistance in cross-border zones, Global LabConnect engineered a decentralized surveillance network connecting district facilities with primary reference hubs. The intervention established standardized specimen transport chains, synchronized digital laboratory information systems, and rapid field sequencing deployments to minimize delay between pathogen detection and public health countermeasures.

coronavirus Key Interventions & Scope of Work

  • check Surveillance Architecture: Deployment of harmonized AMR testing algorithms and clinical diagnostic panels across target sentinel sites.
  • check Field Diagnostic Mobilization: Rapid response surge kits deployed with mobile PCR and rapid diagnostic tests for localized clusters.
  • check Data Interoperability: Direct linkage between laboratory testing registries and national public health epidemiological command databases.
  • check Biosafety & Specimen Custody: Safe triple-packaging containment chains established for high-risk specimen dispatch to reference hubs.
